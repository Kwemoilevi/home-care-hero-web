About Home Care Hero

Home Care Hero is Kampala's trusted platform to find verified caregivers, nurses, nannies and support workers.

We were founded by Levi Kwemoi in Kampala, Uganda to solve one problem: families struggle to find safe, reliable home care quickly.

What We Do

  • Verify caregivers with ID and references
  • Live GPS tracking for safety (tracker.html)
  • Secure payments via MTN MoMo
  • Service across Kampala: Makindye, Nakawa, Kawempe, Rubaga, Central, Wakiso
